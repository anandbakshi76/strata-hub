# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (20 November 2026)

The first release of the Strata launch page. Strata is a fictional smart home hub, made for a design pilot.

- **Meet Strata:** one hub built from modules that snap together, with a short story and three key specs for each module: lights, climate, security and energy.
- **Look inside:** scroll down and the hub comes apart into its four modules, brings each one forward as you read about it, and snaps back together. With reduced motion, or on a device without 3D graphics, you see still images instead and nothing moves.
- **Choose a bundle:** Starter $199, Home $349 and Complete $499. Every bundle includes the hub.
- **Hear when it launches:** register interest with your email and, if you like, the module you care about most. Nothing is sent in this demo, and nothing is stored or tracked.
- **Questions:** six answers, from subscriptions to returns.
- **Works for everyone:** keyboard and screen reader friendly, readable without JavaScript, and fast on phones (main content in about 1.6 seconds).
