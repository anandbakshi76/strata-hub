## What they share

These are notes for UI/UX Pro Max when it writes `DESIGN.md` at Step 0. They are not decisions yet.

- **Mood:** calm and engineered. Lots of empty space, with the product carrying the colour and detail.
- **Type:** one large, heavy display face for the few big headlines, and a plain sans for everything else. Small mono labels only where they carry data, such as specs and readings.
- **Colour:** a mostly neutral ground (warm light grey or deep charcoal) with one accent used for actions only.
- **Layout:** one idea per screen-height section. A centred product with text to one side. Few cards.
- **Motion:** slow and purposeful. The product explodes and reassembles as you scroll, labels appear when their part is in view, and there are no decorative effects. A still version is used for reduced motion.
- **Detail:** thin leader lines for callouts, generous radius on buttons only, and no gradients used as decoration.
