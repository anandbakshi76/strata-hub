# Brand

The full design system is in `DESIGN.md` at the project root: tokens, type, spacing, components, the 3D scene, motion and checks. This page is the short version. If they differ, `DESIGN.md` wins.

Starting point: calm, precise, premium hardware, from the references in `design/references/`.

## Fonts

- Display (hero headline, chapter names, bundles title): Archivo 800
- Headings: Archivo 700
- Body and labels: DM Sans 400 and 500
- Specs, prices and readings: IBM Plex Mono 500
- All self-hosted as WOFF2; no font service.

## Colours

- Primary text: stone-900 #1C1917 on stone-50 #FAFAF9 (16.74:1)
- Accent, for actions only: signal #C2410C (white text on it 5.18:1); hover #9A3412
- On dark chapters: text #E7E5E4, secondary #A8A29E, callouts and focus #FB923C
- Alert: #B91C1C (6.19:1 on stone-50)

## Surfaces

- Light chapters: page #FAFAF9, cards #F5F5F4, hairlines #D6D3D1
- Dark chapters (look inside, the four modules) and footer: #1C1917
- A fixed light, dark, light scheme; no theme toggle.

## Tone

Plain English. Short labels, no jargon. Sentence case. No made-up claims ("Recommended", never "Most popular").
