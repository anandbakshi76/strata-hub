## Module chapters

### Lights

Turns the lights on as you walk in and dims them as the evening comes. Set a scene once and every room follows it. (24 words)

- Lights per hub: Up to 50
- Works with: Matter and Zigbee
- Response time: Under 100 ms

### Climate

Learns when you're home and keeps each room at the temperature you set, within 0.5 °C. No schedules to program. (20 words)

- Temperature range: 5 to 35 °C
- Humidity sensing: 0 to 100%
- Power: Battery-free, from the hub

### Security

Knows when a door opens or the house goes quiet when it shouldn't. Alerts reach your phone in seconds, and recordings stay at home. (24 words)

- Door and window sensors: Up to 30
- Alert time: Under 3 seconds
- Video storage: 64 GB, on the hub

## FAQ

- **Is there a subscription?** No. Everything works without a monthly fee.
- **What does Strata work with?** Lights and plugs that use Matter or Zigbee, and the four Strata modules. The app makes setup easier, but the hub runs without it.
- **What happens to my data?** It stays on the hub in your home. Nothing goes to the cloud unless you turn on remote access.
- **When does it ship?** Strata is a fictional product made for a design pilot, so there is no ship date. Registering shows how the launch page works.
- **Can I return it?** Yes, within 30 days, in its box, for a full refund.
- **How is it powered?** The hub plugs into a wall socket. The modules take their power from the hub, so there are no batteries to change.
